Questions

Regular cleaning, answered.

How much does regular house cleaning cost in South West London?

Our regular cleaning starts from £18 per hour. The exact cost depends on the size of your property, how often you'd like us to visit, and the scope of work agreed at consultation.

How long does a regular cleaning visit take?

Most regular visits run between two and four hours, depending on the size of the property and how much is covered in a single visit. We'll agree a realistic duration with you before the first clean.

Will I have the same cleaner every time?

Yes. We assign a consistent, dedicated team to your household so they get to know your home, your preferences, and your routine, rather than sending different people each visit.

How often can I book regular cleaning?

Regular cleaning is available weekly or fortnightly. If you'd like visits spaced three weeks or more apart, this is treated as deep cleaning rather than regular cleaning, since more time passes between visits.
