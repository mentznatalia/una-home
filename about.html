Our story

Considered care, delivered consistently.

Una Home was built on a simple idea: that a genuinely well-kept home comes down to trust, consistency, and people who take real care — not a rotating cast of strangers and a race to the bottom on price.

Why we exist

Built for households that value their time and their privacy.

South West London is full of cleaning companies competing on speed and price. We built Una Home for households that want something different: a service that feels considered from the first phone call, staffed by people who are properly vetted, insured, and trained — and who stay with your household long enough to actually understand it.

That means fewer, better-trained staff rather than a large rotating pool. It means a real consultation before anyone sets foot in your home, not a generic checklist. And it means a standard that's agreed with you upfront, and maintained visit after visit.

What we stand for

Four things we don't compromise on.

Trust

Vetted & insured, without exception

Every member of staff is reference-checked, experienced, and covered by full insurance before they ever visit a client's home.

Discretion

Quiet, respectful, unobtrusive

We work around your household and your privacy — not the other way round — and we never discuss client homes outside of our own team.

Consistency

The same team, every time

We deliberately keep client rosters small enough that the same staff can return to the same homes, visit after visit.

Craft

A finish worth the trust

We'd rather take on fewer households and do the work properly than grow quickly and dilute the standard our name is built on.

Our standards

What "insured and experienced" actually means here.

These are the standards every member of our team meets before they're assigned to a household — not marketing language, but the baseline we operate to.

Insurance

  • Full public liability insurance on every visit
  • Cover for accidental damage and breakages
  • Clear process for reporting and resolving any issue

Vetting & experience

  • References checked before any household placement
  • Genuine, verified cleaning and housekeeping experience
  • Ongoing training in products, fabrics and surfaces

Consistency

  • The same dedicated team assigned to your household
  • A documented plan agreed with you at consultation
  • Cover arranged in advance if your usual team is away

Discretion

  • Client details kept strictly confidential
  • Keys and access handled with clear, agreed protocols
  • Quiet, considerate work around your schedule
