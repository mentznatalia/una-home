Questions

Housekeeping, answered.

What's the difference between housekeeping and regular cleaning?

Regular cleaning focuses on cleanliness — surfaces, floors, bathrooms. Housekeeping is broader ongoing upkeep: tidying, organising, restocking, and small household tasks that keep daily life running smoothly.

Can housekeeping include restocking household items?

Yes. From a list you provide, we can restock everyday essentials such as toiletries and kitchen supplies as part of a housekeeping visit.

Do you organise wardrobes and storage?

Yes, organising wardrobes, cupboards and storage areas — including seasonal wardrobe rotation — is part of our general housekeeping service.

Is housekeeping available alongside regular cleaning?

Yes, most households combine housekeeping with a regular cleaning schedule so the same trusted team handles both cleanliness and everyday upkeep.
