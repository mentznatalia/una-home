Questions

Deep cleaning, answered.

What's the difference between regular cleaning and deep cleaning?

Regular cleaning is booked weekly or fortnightly to maintain an already well-kept home. Deep cleaning covers visits spaced three weeks or more apart, and goes further — inside ovens, fridges and cupboards, hanging lights and lamps, limescale removal, grout, and areas not covered on a typical visit.

How often should I book a deep clean?

Most households book a deep clean quarterly or seasonally, alongside one-off deep cleans before hosting, after building work, or at a move-in or move-out.

How much does a deep clean cost?

Deep cleaning starts from £22 per hour. The exact cost depends on property size and current condition, confirmed after a short consultation.

How long does a deep clean take?

This depends on property size and current condition — typically a full day for an average family home. We'll confirm timing and pricing after a short consultation.

Can deep cleaning be combined with regular cleaning?

Yes. Many households pair a quarterly deep clean with an ongoing regular cleaning schedule, so the home never drifts far from its best condition.
