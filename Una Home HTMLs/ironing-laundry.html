What's included

Careful with fabric, precise with a press.

Every garment is treated according to what it's made of, not a single default cycle — the same care you'd expect from a good hotel valet service.

Laundry care

  • Garments sorted by fabric type and colour
  • Washed at appropriate temperatures for each load
  • Specialist care for silk, wool, cashmere and linen
  • Tumble-dried or air-dried as the fabric requires
  • Light pre-treatment of marks where safe to do so

Pressing & finishing

  • Shirts pressed with crisp collars and cuffs
  • Trousers, skirts and dresses pressed to shape
  • Delicate fabrics pressed at safe temperatures
  • Bed linen and pillowcases pressed on request
  • Hung or folded ready for wardrobe or drawer

Bed & bath linen

  • Bed linen laundered and pressed
  • Towels laundered and neatly folded
  • Beds changed and made up on the same visit
  • Rotation of linen sets on a schedule you set

Putting away

  • Folding and organising into wardrobes and drawers
  • Arranged by your preferred system, on request
  • Seasonal items stored as directed
  • Delicates handled and hung with care

Questions

Ironing & laundry, answered.

Do you launder delicate fabrics like silk and wool?

Yes. Garments are sorted by fabric and colour, and delicates including silk, wool, and linen are given appropriate care in washing, drying, and pressing.

Can ironing and laundry be added to a regular cleaning visit?

Yes, many households add ironing and laundry to their regular cleaning schedule. It can also be booked as a standalone service.

Do you fold and put clothes away?

Yes, on request. We can fold and organise laundered items into wardrobes and drawers exactly as you'd like them stored.

What's included in bed linen and towel service?

Bed linen and towels are laundered, pressed where appropriate, and can be changed and made up on the bed as part of the same visit.
